O: Obviously, you'd say that. In order not to go to the extent of breaking the civilised code of conduct, you find loopholes. You'll find some or the other living organisms, from the tiniest ant to the blue whale, whatever falls within your power, to torment just because you can. If not in your power, in the end, you will torment yourself, as you will always have power over yourself. Incessantly. By mentally torturing YOURSELF, questioning whether you are a "good" human being or not when, at the crux of it, such a question shouldn't arise, 'cause a human being can never be "good" or "bad", but only an animal. Empathy can't save you as that, too, is just another tool for selective bias, shaped through evolution.

O: Think of man as a dog. Bear with me, just for the sake of an analogy. The only difference between a "good" and a "bad" dog is a leash. Society and religion tend to put the leash on extremely well. They train the dogs how to behave, how to shit, how to live, why NOT to become an animal, and other shit. But a dog will always remain a dog, a slave to its primal instincts.

I: Are you seriously comparing us with dogs to prove a point here? Just say straight up what you're tryna say, man!

O: Power. Power to control the beast inside oneself. Before, there was religion, then there came justice, now there are Billionaires. The ones pulling the leashes will always remain free to exercise their beasts but the common will always be punished.

O: Bruh, I don't care about any bitch-ass person. I only deduce based on my own observations. Also, fuck the people who say that these are merely temptations which one should control and reach higher enlightenment and other utter bullshit. Water cannot renounce the properties that make it itself. Even if it tries to, it can't, and anyone who says it can, is DEFINITELY tryna con ya. 'Cause listen, desires stemming from our egos and driven through power, are a property of ours, we cannot ever escape that shit. Although, I want to clarify here one thing.

I: What's that?

O: I don't want this to be interpreted as extremism. Look, I'm not saying go out and kill someone just for the pleasure of it. That's some "Purge" level shit, please leave that to the movies! Such extent is instead a by-product of suppression by the society. When a catapult is tested to its limits, it'll always fling the farthest shot. Nobody in their right mind would go to such lengths unless they're cooked by the leash on themselves. You gotta release your steam occasionally otherwise it'll fuck you up, you know what I mean?
